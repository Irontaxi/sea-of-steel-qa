Sea of Steel QA

Steam testing 26686375 · Version 2026.11.15.1

Packaged Windows startup passed. Q415 was verified in a controlled nine-ship reproduction. Route searches fell from 410 per five movement substeps to ten on the first attempt and zero once conditions held steady. Peak time in final formation placement fell from 13.9 ms to 0.33 ms. The original match is still open for retest because its live battle save was unavailable.

Naval movement performance

  • Q415 · Move several ships along a coast or through a narrow passage, then let them try to settle at a constrained final waypoint. Watch for a repeating hitch roughly four times a second while ships regroup. If it recurs, include the scenario or seed, the order, and a performance trace.
  • Q415 · Replace a fleet’s constrained waypoint with a reachable one while it is regrouping. Check that ships take the new order, continue around land, and recover their stations.

AA escorts, shorelines and mines

  • Q418 · Deploy the U.S. and Japanese AA escorts. Check that each has its own faction hull and that AA fire originates at the visible gun mounts.
  • Q418 · Compare the same incoming air raid with and without an AA escort. Check the escort engages aircraft and offers useful protection. Present a surface target as well and verify the escort has no surface battery.
  • Q419 · Generate a fresh campaign or Coral Sea map. Inspect a beach and a narrow channel at tactical zoom. Check the submerged shelf blends into deeper water, the land shoreline stays intact, and a fleet can cross the channel and continue across the sector edge.
  • Q417 / Q420 · Inspect the U.S. and Japanese minelayer and minesweeper hulls. After setup in Open Ocean, select one Minesweeper and run Sea.Mines.TestField in the in-game console. A laid friendly mine should show a friendly hazard marker with its prop submerged. A forward mine detected by Sweep should surface and bob.
  • Q420 · In open water, order a friendly ship past a friendly mine on a route that would cross it. Repeat with an enemy mine revealed by Sweep. Verify the ship steers clear of each marked mine, takes no mine damage and continues toward its destination.
  • Q420 · Leave Sweep active and save with friendly and revealed enemy mines in the water. Reload and verify Sweep stays active, the mines are still at the saved positions, and their markers match their saved state.
  • Q417 · Spend some mines, then bring the minelayer and a fleet supply ship together and stop. Verify that mine stock rises toward 24, supplier ammunition falls, and laying stays off until you turn it back on.

Campaign, tutorials and save reload

  • Q401 · Start a new campaign and prepare Sector 01. Confirm the preview marks clear north and west ingress lanes and leaves the interior borders unmarked.
  • Q401 · Continue to Fleet Builder and press Deploy Fleet. Confirm the picker offers only clear north and west lanes that fit your fleet. Choose an offered lane and verify the fleet enters there.
  • Q403 · Open Tutorials and start Carrier Basics. Follow the on-screen instructions. At a smaller window size, confirm the tutorial card and its navigation buttons fit. Complete Carrier Basics, then use Next Tutorial to reach Ship Handling and Naval Gunnery in order.
  • Q403 · Complete all three tutorials. Exit and relaunch through Steam, then confirm all three lessons still show completed.
  • Q416 · Save while a destroyed hull is still sinking and another wreck has already settled. Reload while paused. Check that the descending hull is in its saved pose, the settled hull stays submerged, and both wreck markers remain.

Ground audio, controls and airfields

  • Move a tank, then stop and traverse its turret. Check engine and track sounds during movement, the transition at a stop, and turret sound during traverse.
  • Move an infantry squad, halt, then let it engage. Check footsteps during movement and the existing rifle sound during combat. Observe an AA engagement and listen for the flak audio.
  • Select a carrier and then an airfield. Use the Aviation plus and minus controls. Check that each icon and count is readable, each click changes the intended count, and the next launch uses that count from the selected base.
  • Q332 · Preview, build, and select a field MG nest. Confirm both cones face the gun and span 90° (45° per side) out to 48 m. With clear visibility, confirm the nest tracks and fires automatically at incoming infantry around 40° to either side. Check it holds fire outside the cone or beyond 48 m, pauses after four bursts, and resumes after reloading.
  • Inspect a newly generated airfield objective with support batteries. Verify the batteries sit inland, clear of the runway and starting fleets. Record the seed and objective if a battery blocks a route or threatens a starting fleet.
  • Inspect the roofs on hangars at a generated airfield. Pan around them and zoom in and out. Check for missing roof sections, holes, or obvious clipping.
  • Launch several Japanese Zero fighters and inspect their liveries at close zoom. Check that the Akutan variant appears in normal play and that its paint and markings render correctly.

Remote Steam co-op

  • Q352 · Join remotely over Steam with both players on this build. Launch aircraft from a guest-controlled carrier and check the guest sees aircraft models and flight markers.
  • Q352 · Compare moving ships on both screens. Verify guest hulls and map markers move smoothly between updates.
  • Q352 · With at least two detected contacts, press Tab as guest. Verify contact selection advances and the display identifies the selected contact.
  • Q352 · Reveal an enemy contact through friendly scouting. Compare both screens and verify the guest receives the shared contact while enemies outside friendly detection stay hidden.
  • Q352 · Send a map ping from the host, then from the guest. Verify each ping appears at the correct location on the other screen.
  • Q352 · Have the guest leave normally. Verify the host sees the disconnect status and can continue the battle.
