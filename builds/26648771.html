Q398 logistics priority checks

  • Select a ship and check that the HUD shows health, fuel, and ammo. Fire a salvo and confirm the red shell counter reflects the ammunition spent.
  • In Scenarios, select United States and Open Ocean, enable enemy fleets, then choose Generate → Continue to Fleet Builder. Add a Fleet supply ship and a Destroyer and deploy. Verify the U.S. supply ship model. After setup, use Attack Location to fire into nearby water while outside supply range. Stop both ships within 50 m with the simulation running. Confirm destroyer fuel and red shell count rise and supplier stores fall.
  • Repeat the Open Ocean resupply test as Japan. Verify the Japanese supply ship model and confirm the same fuel and ammunition transfer.
  • With a depleted recipient, check that replenishment requires both ships to be within 50 m and moving slowly or stopped. Confirm the supplier draws down finite stores and stops transferring a resource when its stock runs out.
  • After resupply, record both ships’ stock levels. Save and reload the battle and verify the exact remaining destroyer fuel and ammunition and supplier stocks.
  • In a career transit, confirm stationed ships replenish at the campaign step and crossing ships keep their remaining stocks.
  • Empty a ship’s main-gun magazines and run fuel low. Confirm naval guns stop firing, reserve propulsion remains available, and AA can still fire without consuming ammunition.
