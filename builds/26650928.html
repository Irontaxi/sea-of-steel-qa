Sea of Steel QA

Steam testing 26650928 · Version 2026.11.13.2

Priority checks

  • Host a co-op battle. At the bottom of Command View, check that status shows Listening before the guest joins and Guest Connected after they join.
  • On the guest client, try a connection that times out. Check that connecting and timeout feedback appear, then retry with the correct address and confirm the connection succeeds.
  • Connect a second player by direct IP and confirm both players enter the same battle.
  • Reload an existing career and resume a saved battle. Open Command View and check that every campaign sector is shown.
  • Launch the game and review the replacement startup intro. Check that cannon audio plays in sync and the main menu opens afterward.

Open reproductions

  • Q404 · Open reproduction. Send a carrier/cruiser formation at 50 m spread through a wide strait with queued shoreline waypoints. Record the seed and waypoint locations if ships remain turning, blocked, or yielding.
  • Recheck the film grain filter in battle. Verify the UI stays usable and Escape leaves controls responsive.

Film grain and Escape feedback came from report #17 on the preceding build.

Outstanding reviews

Continued from the previous checklist. Career transit checks cover solo naval transit.

  • In a career transit, confirm stationed ships replenish at the campaign step and crossing ships keep their remaining stocks.
  • Empty a ship’s main-gun magazines and run fuel low. Confirm naval guns stop firing, reserve propulsion remains available, and AA can still fire without consuming ammunition.
  • In a solo career, win a sector and choose Continue Playing. Order transit and send one ship to the exit. Leave a second ship and landed units in the origin. Select Complete Transit. Confirm only the ship that reached the exit arrives at the matching ingress with its condition and ammo, and the world advances one step.
  • Revisit the origin sector. Confirm the ship and landed units left behind restore in place with their condition.
  • Save and quit a live scenario. Resume from Scenarios and verify the clock is paused and units and queued orders are restored.
  • Save and quit a live career battle. Resume via Career → Campaign Operations → Load Saved Battle. Verify the career’s save list, paused clock, units, and queued orders. Confirm unfinished play adds no career stats and does not advance the campaign.
  • In a career battle, open Command View and inspect neighboring sector previews. Check fog and revealed areas.
  • Deploy and check tactical command icons. Collapse and reopen Aviation.
  • In a timed scenario, complete the required objective with an optional objective still open. Confirm main objectives sort first and victory opens the summary and Battle Stats.
  • Check that ship impacts show effects and play sound.
  • In co-op, hand off a ship in Command View and verify order ownership. Compare fog and explored areas on host and guest.
  • Observe enemy resupply and an airfield counterattack.
  • Open Land Warfare Lab through Terrain Preset. Land tanks and deploy infantry landing craft from a large troop transport. Check that the craft can unload and respond to move or cancel orders.
  • Compare confirmed impacts on a Japanese frigate with hull loss. Record the seed and contact state if damage stalls.
  • With fresh preferences, check that Deploy, CAP, and Focus each highlight once.
  • Run a busy seed 9 battle and capture a short trace if it stutters. Report timing and conditions in Notes. Keep raw traces and logs private.
