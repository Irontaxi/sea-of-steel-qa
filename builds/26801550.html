  • Sortie · Play a full match against the bot with each starter faction. Pay the displayed Fuel, Ammo and Resources costs and exercise deployment, attack, interception and response controls. Verify legal actions progress the match to its ending and unavailable actions explain their requirements. Record the faction, phase and card if play stalls.
  • Sortie · Play a local two-seat match. At each handoff, check the incoming player must confirm Ready before their hand appears. Check that the opposing hand stays concealed, including when inspecting a crowded battlefield and after returning to Sortie.
  • Sortie · Close and reopen Sortie in the same running game. Verify the current match resumes. Start New Match and verify a fresh match replaces it without stale selections or response controls. Record any card action that triggers after changing matches.
  • Interface size · In Settings, change Interface size from 75% to 150% using the offered 5% steps at 1280 × 720 and a larger resolution. Verify preview, readability, button hit targets and final fit. Close Settings and restart to check the chosen value persists. In co-op, confirm host and guest values remain independent.
  • Land selection · Assign land groups with Shift plus 1–9 and recall them by number. Check the header shows the selected count and a quick second recall glides toward surviving members. Inspect the first row of shared orders and the applicable ability rows for one squad, several squads and a tank.
  • Infantry grenades · Use the offered grenade control at a visible dry-land target within range. Check limited stock, a visible throw and impact, and that invalid or hidden targets are rejected. Keep friendly units clear, then deliberately test friendly-fire behavior in a disposable battle.
  • Infantry Dig In · Drag the facing on reachable suitable ground and commit. Verify the squad moves into a continuous trench with the requested orientation. Repeat with a reduced squad and check the surviving soldiers spread across the trench.
  • Reinforcements · Switch All, Ships, Land Units and Planes. Verify each list and quote matches the filter, including a legitimately empty Land Units list. Confirm a purchase and check the offered ingress and destination controls complete the deployment.
  • Career Combat Recon · In an eligible sector as each faction, buy the quoted 450-resource package. Verify it supplies three faction aircraft (U.S. P-38L or Japanese Ki-45), follows the chosen edge ingress and patrol/screen area, and charges once.
  • Aviation · With an eligible carrier and aircraft ready, launch a new sortie. Select an existing flight and redirect it through the flight controls. Check new launch orders create a fresh flight while redirecting retains the selected one. Check dive/torpedo bomber selection before a strike.
  • Mine ownership · On this build, turn a minelayer through a mine it just placed and verify that exact hull is immune. Use a different friendly hull to confirm the mine remains dangerous to other ships. Save and reload on the same build and repeat the ownership check.
  • Fleet supply · Bring supplier and recipient within 75 m, slow or stop both and run the simulation. Verify finite fuel, ammunition and mine stocks transfer, then stop at exhaustion. Compare U.S. and Japanese specialist hull visuals during the transfer.
  • Submarine depth · Select a submarine and cycle Surface, Periscope and Deep. Verify the control shows the current mode and requests the next one, with depth and battery restrictions enforced. Check a mixed selection uses the first eligible submarine.
  • Tactical labels · With Labels On select one live unit and verify passive firing and vision ranges. Select several and check the overlays clear. Issue a move and verify route crosses and lines remain while the DEST text is absent.
  • Audio output · In Settings → Audio, switch between System Default and an available playback endpoint. Verify game playback follows the selected output and microphone selection remains independent.
