Sea of Steel QA

Steam testing 26573645 · Version 2026.11.10.1

  • Q381 · Cards and icons · Assign numbered groups containing ships of different classes, land units, and mixed ship/land members. Check semi-transparent cards show each group number and the strongest available member’s Stats-style icon. Remove or lose that member and check the icon updates; empty or unavailable groups should have no card.
  • Q381 · Order and selection · Assign groups out of order. Check cards run left to right by group number. Click each card to select its group; double-click to center the camera on it.
  • Q381 · Viewport clearance · At normal and narrow window sizes, check cards remain usable and clear of utility buttons, Task Force, footer and menus. Start a formation drag and check its prompt stays readable and separate from the cards.
  • Q365 · Radio footer review · Expand Radio Log and scroll back. Click the bottom chevron/footer to return to the newest report and resume live-follow. Check the footer stays below history, consumes the click without map orders, and disappears when collapsed.
  • Q374 · Fort aim and arcs review · Observe an offshore fort firing at seaward targets. Check barrels aim at the target and respect structural limits: the upper turret can fire directly aft, the lower main turret can fire past either beam but not directly aft, and casemates stay within their openings.
  • Q374 · Main gun strength review · Observe offshore fort main-gun hits on ships. Check they feel like capital-ship weapons, with a slower heavy salvo cadence; record target class and observed damage if unexpectedly weak. This reviews gun strength, not the already-approved concrete body.
  • Q373 · First-time guidance · With fresh preferences, check brief outlines on Deploy, CAP and Focus as each first becomes relevant. Restart and confirm shown highlights do not repeat.
  • Q358 / Q377 / Q380 · Victory and finish · In scenario and career battles, finish main objectives with optional ones active or failed. Check one delayed victory cue and named summary with destroyed units, objectives and resources. Finish Battle should open the just-ended Replay → Battle Stats with export.
  • Q377 / Q380 · Continue and outcomes · Choose Continue Playing after victory, earn an optional reward, then exit and reload the career. Check progress and rewards save once. Compare required-airfield capture retained versus enemy recapture before fleet defeat. Check finalized defeat and withdrawal also open the correct Replay → Battle Stats.
  • Q364 · Land Warfare Lab · Open Land Warfare Lab. Check boat approach and beach landing, disembark infantry, and bring amphibious tanks ashore. Record any grounding, blocked approach or failed unload.
  • Performance · Seed 9 · Play a busy seed 9 battle with normal visual defaults. If it chugs, use Debug → Start Trace and Stop Trace for a short capture. Record conditions and timing in notes; keep raw traces and local logs out of public issues.
