Main guns and AA

Map scale: one chart-grid square is 300 × 300 tactical meters in standard sectors. Compare weapon range with the width of a square: 300 m is one square, 150 m is half a square, and 75 m is a quarter square. A battleship’s 275 m gun range is about 0.92 squares; a frigate’s 125 m range is about 0.42 squares. These distances are measured from the firing ship, not across the diameter of a range ring.

Shells per salvo is the gameplay projectile count. National art can show a different number of barrels. AA mounts is the cap per visual burst, limited further by fitted mounts and firing arcs. Nominal ship AA engagement distance is 75 m.

How to read damage in a test

  1. Check whether the guns can fire. Check range, line of sight, posture, reload, gun alignment and contact freshness. A stale report can produce an uncertain aim point.
  2. Count confirmed impacts. Each shell rolls for a valid solution and disperses around the predicted intercept. Distance, flight time and report uncertainty widen the spread. Rough seas reduce the firing solution probability by up to 10% relative. Shell endpoints are fixed at discharge. A target that turns or accelerates can move clear. A narrow hull presents less room for a hit.
  3. Calculate direct shell damage. Raw per-shell damage is base salvo damage × current weapons fraction ÷ shells per salvo. Each valid shell whose endpoint lands inside the target hull applies raw shell damage × (1 − target armor). For example, a 100-point shell against 20% armor removes 80 hull points.
  4. Watch the next few seconds. A hit can damage propulsion, guns, sensors or a flight deck and can add fire, flooding and crew losses. Fire and flooding can reduce hull points after the impact. Repair can offset part of the loss. In advanced combat, weapon condition also lengthens main gun reload up to four times the base interval.

For an apparent no-damage report, capture the build, seed, shooter and target class, estimated range, before/after hull, and whether the target was visually confirmed. Include a short clip if you can. Count individual impacts separately from gun flashes and water splashes.
