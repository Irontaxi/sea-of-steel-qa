# Sea of Steel · Earning resources in a round

Main and optional objectives pay resources when they complete. You can spend those resources on reinforcements during the same battle. In Career, the balance you keep after finishing the deployment is available for later sectors.

## Starting balance

- **Scenario.** The fleet point budget you choose funds your starting fleet and air wings. Anything you leave unspent is available for reinforcements once you deploy. This balance belongs to that scenario.
- **Career.** You bring your available career resources into the sector after paying for any new ships or aircraft in your starting force. Ships you already own still count toward the deployment limit.

## Objective payouts

Open **Objectives** to see the tasks for this sector. Main objectives decide victory. Optional objectives earn extra resources and can remain active or fail without blocking a win. The map and mission types determine which objectives appear.

| Objective | Resources | What earns the payout |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Defeat the sector fleet | 1,000 | Account for all required ships through confirmed losses or a rout. |
| Defeat an enemy patrol | 500 | Account for the ships assigned to that patrol objective. |
| Eliminate a submarine patrol | 500 | Confirm the loss of every required submarine in the patrol. |
| Intercept an enemy convoy | 500 | Confirm the loss of the required cargo ships before they escape or the deadline expires. |
| Escort allied cargo to safety | 750 | Get at least one cargo ship to the convoy exit before the deadline. |
| Capture an airfield | 750 | Take control of the airfield with infantry. |
| Clear a standalone coastal defence | 250 | Capture the site or confirm it has been put out of action. |
| Clear a Coastal Defences area | 250 per required bunker | Clear every listed bunker. The area pays as a single objective. |
| Disable an offshore fort | 1,000 | Disable both main turrets and both side casemates. |

These are the standard generated-sector payouts. A sector with only one eligible mission type can make one of those objectives the main objective. The payout follows the objective assigned to that encounter.

Individual kills count toward the applicable objective and appear in battle statistics. Resources pay when the objective reaches its required count. Each objective pays once, including across repeat deployments to the same career sector.

## Spend or carry the balance forward

The **Radio Log** reports objective payouts. The **Reinforcements** panel shows the available balance and the cost of the selected request. Confirming a request charges the balance. Cancel a request while it is still queued to recover its cost.

On victory, the summary shows resources earned from objectives in that deployment. Choose **Continue Playing** to pursue remaining optional objectives, or **Finish Battle** to settle the round. The first time you secure a career sector, settlement adds a separate sector capture bonus. Campaign rules set its amount.

If you withdraw before victory, take your surviving ships to the map edge and finish the deployment to bank completed objective progress and the remaining balance. Leaving without saving forfeits progress from the unfinished deployment.
